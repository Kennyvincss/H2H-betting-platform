What is Hey Research Lab?

It's the builder-research layer for Robinhood Chain. Instead of tracking price, it tracks work. HEY reads public evidence: code repositories, tagged releases, deployed contracts, packages, docs and a project's own website. From that it records what each project actually ships.

The rule that makes it different: every ship has a source. Click any entry and you land on the release, the commit week or the contract creation it came from.

HEY's own line says it best:
"Price tells you what the market is doing. HEY tells you what the builder is doing."

3/
The honesty layer is what sold me.

Every line on @HeyResearch is labelled:
• Fact: read directly from a source
• Derived: calculated from facts, with the rule shown
• Unknown: HEY couldn't read it

And unknown is never counted as zero. If a project has no repo or feed linked, HEY says "Activity unknown". That's different from "Dormant", which means HEY looked at a real source and saw no work.

Hype, giveaways and marketing posts are recorded but never counted as building. Activity is never derived from price, market cap, volume, holders or wallets. Trading is not building.

6/
The metrics, and why they can't be gamed:

📈 Build Momentum: a score built from shipping evidence only. A product launch weighs more than a docs update, announcements count zero, and aggregated commit activity is capped, so neither commit spam nor marketing can inflate it. No market figure ever reaches it.

🔭 Discovery Gap: compares how actively a project ships with how much market attention its token gets.

👀 Under the Radar ("Building, little attention"): active, Build Momentum ≥30, real ships in the last 30 days, and a positive Discovery Gap.

🛠 Still Building: the token is at or below half its 90-day high, but the team kept shipping after the drop.

None of these are buy signals. They're context.
