What is Hey Research Lab?

It's the builder-research layer for Robinhood Chain. Most tools track price. HEY tracks work.

It reads public evidence (code repositories, tagged releases, deployed contracts, packages, docs and a project's own website) and records what every project actually ships. Every ship has a source you can open, so nothing is "trust me".

It's honest about what it knows. Every line is marked Fact (read from a source), Derived (calculated, with the rule shown) or Unknown. Unknown is never counted as zero. If HEY has nothing to read, it says "Activity unknown" instead of pretending a project is dead.

Hype, giveaways and marketing posts are never counted as building, and activity is never read from price, volume or holders.

"Price tells you what the market is doing. HEY tells you what the builder is doing."

The metrics, and why they can't be gamed:

📈 Build Momentum: built from shipping evidence only. A product launch weighs more than a docs update, announcements count zero, and commit volume is capped, so spam and marketing can't inflate it. No market figure reaches it.

🔭 Discovery Gap: compares how actively a project ships with how much market attention its token gets.

👀 Under the Radar: steady builders with a positive gap, the "building, little attention" list.

🛠 Still Building: teams that kept shipping after their token fell to half its 90-day high or lower.

None of these are buy signals. They're context. And HEY holds itself to the same rules: its own project page is researched exactly like everyone else's, and $HEY can never buy a ranking, a status or coverage.
